更新纪录

游戏中所有的变更，依更新排列，最新的在最前面。每次更新都以重点开头。

2026年11月22日

你的进度安全无虞

  • 过时的游戏版本（旧分页、没有重新加载的 App）同步时不会再抹掉你的进度。
  • 捕获的传说宝可梦与传说之路的进度（基格尔德的细胞与内核、科斯莫古、高塔、六角螺帽）即使旧设备同步也会留在账号上。
  • 旧版本不认得的内容，现在会与账号保存的数据合并，而不是取而代之。
  • 每只传说宝可梦的固有特性现在都会说明效果：在宝可梦详情、暂停菜单、道场和网站图鉴中。
所有变更（2）

账号同步

  • A device still running an old version of the game didn’t know the newest legends and legend paths and, when it synced, could leave your account without them. Now every sync says which version of the game it comes from, and one from a version older than the last that saved your account is always merged with what’s kept, taking nothing away.
  • A sync missing a legend capture, or with less legend path progress than your account has, is merged with what’s kept too.

传说宝可梦

  • The innate (Articuno’s Snow Cloak, Jirachi’s Serene Grace…) shows the ability’s text and what the Legend mode adds to it, like the chill of Articuno’s wings. In the pause menu it shows from Lv 1, beside the ability picked at the first awakening.

2026年11月21日

以你的币别购买宝石

  • 宝石包现在会以你的币别显示价格（美元、欧元、新台币、披索…），付款也以该币别扣款。
  • 同一个付款页面就有全部方式：信用卡、Apple Pay、Google Pay，在巴西还有 Pix。
  • 宝石不够买外观吗？它的按钮会直接打开能补足差额的宝石包。
  • 宝石商店现在会显示 Patreon 每月赞助：每个等级每月都会带来宝石，已链接的玩家会看到自己的等级。
  • 黑色星期五 11 月 27 日至 30 日：所有付费宝石包多送 50% 宝石。
  • 登录后，结算画面会显示当天的免费宝石，以及你的余额已经买得起的外观，点一下就到服饰店。
所有变更（4）

购买宝石

  • Outside Brazil, the Shop shows an approximate price in your device’s currency, and the checkout page shows and charges the exact amount in your currency. In Brazil nothing changes: the same prices in reais, now with Pix on the same page as cards.
  • Backed out of a payment? You land back in the Boutique, which offers the same pack again and the other ways to pay, instead of the title screen.
  • A look you can’t afford yet shows on its own button how many gems you’re short and the pack that covers them, with its price; tapping opens that pack’s checkout.

支持游戏

  • The gem shop has a Patreon card with every tier’s gems a month and what supporters get. Patrons who linked Patreon see their own tier instead, and the Hall of Fame and the Support page now say the gems a month too.
  • One-time donations now go through Stripe, from Brazil and abroad, in a single form: Pix and cards in reais, and donors from other countries see the amount in their own currency.
  • After a few milestones (25 runs, a first Nightmare win, 100 runs), the lobby asks once whether you’d like to support the game. Supporters never see it.

黑色星期五

  • From November 27 to 30 (Brasília time), every paid pack brings 50% more gems for the same price. The welcome pack is left out: it already gives twice as many. The Shop, the gem counter and the Boutique in the lobby show the tag and how long is left.
  • What counts is what the Shop showed when you opened the checkout: a pack opened during Black Friday and paid later still brings the extra gems.

免费宝石

  • The day’s first run of 5 minutes or more gives 15 gems, and playing 5 days in a week gives 35 more. The results now show when they land and your balance, with the cheapest look you don’t own yet (or the Vitrine do dia’s) that you can already buy and a button that opens the Boutique on it; if none yet, how many gems are left to go.
  • The lobby’s gem counter glows when your balance covers a look you don’t own yet, until you visit the Boutique.

合作模式的传说宝可梦与异兽提升修正

  • 合作模式也能玩传说模式：房主在房间打开后，每人使用自己捕获的传说宝可梦，敌群更强，还有压迫感与觉醒。
  • 新排行榜：传说双人、三人与四人小队，位于排行榜的传说合作页。
  • 只有账号里捕获过的传说宝可梦才算：还没捕获任何一只的玩家，无法在传说模式房间准备。
  • 异兽提升（究极异兽）与自信过度：刚获得的层数不会再立刻消失。
  • 只有连续 4 秒没有击倒（自信过度为 1 秒）才会掉一层，连续击倒就能叠到上限。
  • 排名对局改用新版规则，旧版规则的对局仍然有效。
所有变更（2）

Legend Mode in co-op

  • In a co-op room, the host has a new Legend Mode option. With it on, each player picks one of the legends they captured, and the run plays by Legend Mode’s rules: a tougher horde, Pressure and awakenings at Lv 12 and 28 (Primal Kyogre and Groudon, Mega Rayquaza).
  • Only legends captured on your account count: the server checks as the run starts. A player who hasn’t captured any can’t get ready in a Legend Mode room.
  • Legendary runs have their own boards in the Ranking, on the Legendary Co-op side. The daily co-op Challenge stays in the standard mode, and epic rifts are still fought in the old arena in co-op.

修正

  • Beast Boost (Ultra Beasts) and Moxie: the stack you just earned no longer vanishes at once; a stack only drops after 4 s without KOs (1 s for Moxie).

2026年11月18日

暗影血清与不设限的游戏城

  • 净化暗影宝可梦前会先确认，并写出牠的名字，提醒暗影型态会消失。
  • 游戏城喵喵柜台新上架：暗影血清，可让你选的一只净化宝可梦变回暗影。
  • 不小心净化了暗影超梦？血清能让暗影型态回来；想再净化，就要重新走满心门量表。
  • 游戏城不再打烊：拉霸机和瓦斯弹扫雷想玩多久就玩多久，每天最多可买 5,000 枚代币（原本是 500）。
  • 游戏城柜台添加异色奖品：Ditto、Absol、Mawile、Gimmighoul、两只 Nidoran、Cleffa、Plusle 和 Minun 都有异色版了。
  • 已经有 Rotom 或 Wishiwashi 了吗？再次找到牠们时有小概率得到异色：废弃小屋的旧电视和离码头很远的钓鱼都有机会。
所有变更（4）

Shinies on repeat finds

  • If you already own Rotom, each run where the old TV crackles has a 1 in 20 chance of letting out a shiny Rotom. It arrives in the gift box.
  • If you already own Wishiwashi, each fishing session that hooks another has a 1 in 25 chance of bringing a shiny Wishiwashi. It arrives in the gift box.

Game Corner

  • The slots no longer have a spin limit or a vault that closes: every spin pays what it lands. Three Poké Balls now pay 10 (was 12) and three Magikarp 7 (was 9); the big prizes are the same.
  • Koffing Sweep no longer has a board limit. It pays in full up to 1,500 tokens a day and half up to 2,250, then goes into free play: boards still count for your level, they just give no tokens until the next day.
  • Meowth’s counter sells up to 5,000 tokens a day, with a new pack of 1,000 tokens for ₽20,000.

Shadow Pokémon

  • The Purify button opens a confirmation (Cancel comes selected) that names the Pokémon and says its Shadow form is lost, unless you use the Shadow Serum.
  • The Shadow Serum costs 11,000 tokens at the prize counter and can be traded for as often as you like. It arrives in the gift box, and opening it turns the chosen Pokémon Shadow again, its heart closed.
  • Every counter family has a shiny prize, from 7,500 tokens (Ditto) to 15,000 (Cleffa). It arrives in the gift box and hatches the family shiny; if you already had the family, it turns shiny. If you already traded for the regular family, you only pay the difference.

Fixes

  • Bibarel’s Carpentry said "In your collection" on every mini game and Stamps trophy, even ones you didn’t have. Now each says what earns it, like "Score 400 points in Pier Fishing".

所有变更（4）

第一次玩？Rotom 带你认识游戏

  • 新玩家可能会遇到 Rotom 迎接，花大约 2 分钟边玩边教基本操作：移动、自动施放的招式、经验值、强化、精灵球和头目。随时都能跳过，或点「我会玩了」。
  • 接着从各地区的 27 只御三家中选出第一位伙伴。在设置 > 游戏里的「重看教学」可以再看一次，还会带你逛一圈大厅。

Apple Pay and Google Pay for donations in Brazil

  • On the Support page, donations in reais (AbacatePay) now take Apple Pay and Google Pay, besides Pix and card.

修正

  • Runs wouldn’t start on Chrome 109 and earlier (Windows 7 and 8, older Chromebooks) since Season 1. Fixed.
  • Players stuck on “Loading…” on their first visit (mostly in mainland China and Hong Kong) now get into the game.
  • Hoopa’s Rings: beating a boss’s helper no longer skips to the next ring.
  • The Tera crystal’s shatter no longer errors in co-op, and a crystal with no facets no longer breaks the transformation.
  • In Android in-app browsers, a Pokémon’s cry no longer crashes the game.
  • Mega Stones and boss loot that fell into Lugia’s whirlpool or Groudon’s lava lake now land on the nearest ground.
  • A shiny legend beaten before the capture now unlocks the playable shiny. Saves in that state get it when the game opens.
  • Picking up an X Attack or X Speed during a long altar buff no longer shortens the buff.
  • On iPhone in portrait, the Start button no longer covers the Bag before a run.
  • The Stamp Book opened from the Trainer Card closes with Back again.
  • In co-op, leveling up with everything maxed no longer flashes the screen.
  • The palm tree’s shadow on the Poké Realm pier now sits under it.

调整

  • Electro Shot (Duraludon): damage 16 → 28, cooldown 2.6 → 2.2 s, a bigger beam and the rain bonus ×1.2 → ×1.5.
  • iPhone: “Play with other apps” in Settings → Sound keeps other apps’ podcasts and music playing with the game (on silent, the game goes quiet).
  • Clearer texts: the mythicals’ chance at the 15:00 finale, what happens to a wild shiny you beat, and what Forget does.
  • Galarian Ponyta now sits with the other Galarian forms: it shows in the partner select’s Galar section and hatches from the Travel Egg in Galar’s week (no longer from the Rare Egg). If you have it, you keep it.
  • The Daily Challenge board of 11/17 mixed Stufful and Starly: the day’s Pokémon changed mid-day. The next days won’t change anymore. Sorry!

2026年11月16日

第 1 赛季：太晶化、全国图鉴和新的传说

  • 全国图鉴登场：745 种宝可梦加入怪潮，还有 104 个新的可玩家族，各有独特机制。
  • 新的传说：帕路奇亚、10 只究极异兽、湖之三神、自然之力、雷吉艾勒奇和雷吉铎拉戈、蒂安希、捷拉奥拉、厄鬼椪和玛纳霏。传说模式共有 62 只。
  • 23 种新的超级进化，从超级赫拉克罗斯到超级皮可西：现在共有 59 条进化线、66 种超级进化。
  • 太晶化：从头目身上收集太晶碎块，集满太晶珠，戴上 19 种皇冠之一化为结晶。每局都有太晶团体战，还有每日团体战和太乐巴戈斯。
  • Poké Realm 里：火箭队的游戏城，还有狩猎地带限定的家族，像翅膀是你所在地区花纹的彩粉蝶。
  • 大厅添加收音机：自己选音乐、排播放队列。大厅、Poké Realm、狩猎地带、太晶团体战和道场也都有了新音乐。
所有变更（19）

为手机重新设计的伙伴选择

  • 手机直拿时，宝可梦面板变得精简，清单占满其余画面；横拿时，清单占满整个高度，难度和开始按钮就在旁边，伸手可及。分页的数量改显示在名称下方，不再有内容超出画面。
  • 在传说分页中，Hoopa 的圆环横幅现在会随清单一起卷动：在手机上可以滑到任何一只传说宝可梦。
  • 未解锁的传说宝可梦会更清楚地说明取得方式：每把正义之剑会写出牠等待的地区和考验内容，Cosmog 会说明星空研究在哪里、需要几场游戏，Meltan 则说明最多 12 面岩壁内一定能挖到 Mystery Box。
  • Urshifu 的两种流派：在道馆之塔，爬过一座塔的人现在可以爬另一座，并随时在塔的卡片上切换一击流和连击流。

第 1 赛季与第 1 纪元名人堂

  • The run rankings get seasons: the main tab is now Season, which opens fresh on November 16. Nothing is erased: the records from before stay forever in Era 1, a Hall of Fame with its own podium.
  • Era 1’s top players are rewarded: each ranking’s #1 gets the Era 1 Champion title, the Crown frame and the Throne sky, and the next ones at the top get the Era 1 Legend title and the Era 1 frame. The gifts arrive on their own after the turnover.
  • Endless mode tightens after 30 minutes: the horde grows faster and faster, and the best builds now fall between 45 and 60 minutes instead of lasting hours.
  • Tweaks to the unbeatable builds: Magic Guard only halves damage from blasts and pools, Bulletproof blocks 40%, cooldown cuts pay less past 30% and Iron Head guards for less time.

太晶化与太晶团体战

  • Every boss leaves two Tera Shards: its type and one of your Pokémon’s types. Walk to the one you want to pick your Tera type.
  • The Tera Orb fills with KOs and with time. Once full, press TERA (R): your Pokémon turns to crystal under its type’s crown for 20 seconds, and moves of that type hit harder. Stellar triples the first hit of each move type.
  • There are 19 crystal crowns, one per type plus Stellar, each with its own shape, sitting on the Pokémon’s head (small heads wear a smaller cut). The transformation has the crystal growing, a slow-motion shatter and a full-screen banner.
  • Tera Raids: a Tera Den opens on the map halfway through every run, solo or co-op. Inside, a giant crystal boss with a Tera shield: break it with its weakness or your Tera type. Later the Raid of the Day opens, the same for everyone, and on weekends it’s a 7★ Stellar Terapagos.
  • Beat the 7★ Stellar Raid to capture Terapagos and play it in Legend mode: it awakens into its Terastal Form and then its Stellar Form, with Tera Starstorm.

全国图鉴

  • The horde grows from 415 to 745 species: almost every Pokémon there is shows up in the world. Rare ones stay rare, and each biome pulls in its own types.
  • The in-game Pokédex is now the National one, region by region, from Kanto to Paldea: 909 of the 1,025 Pokémon are in the game, plus 43 forms (42 regional ones and Eternal Floette). Complete a region to earn its Researcher title, and all nine for the National Pokédex frame.
  • The Pokémon SpriteCollab’s artists are still drawing show in the Pokédex with a thank-you and a link to help draw them.
  • On the website, the Pokédex now has a page per region.

104 个新家族

  • There are now 194 playable families: the 27 starters and 167 from eggs, the Safari Zone, the Mine Dig, encounters on the map and prizes. Each new family has a signature move with paths at Lv 4 and 7, three abilities and a mechanic of its own.
  • From eggs: Snom, Joltik, Salandit, Hatenna, Darumaka, Wimpod, Applin (with Dipplin, Flapple and Appletun), Finizen, Pawniard, Frigibax, Ponyta and Galarian Ponyta. From the new Fossil Egg (Old Amber): Aerodactyl, with a Mega.
  • Found other ways: Rotom in the old TV of abandoned houses, Castform in bad weather, Wishiwashi in the Realm’s fishing, Mimikyu, Morpeko and Tandemaus in the Safari Zone, and Mankey (up to Annihilape), Goomy and Tatsugiri through the new Research tab in Missions.
  • 30 more with mechanics that change the game: Jangmo-o pays HP for Clangorous Soul, Axew stacks Dragon Dance into Guillotine, Onix drags a stone body that coils up, Happiny lays Lucky Eggs that heal teammates too, Sandile dives into the sand, Stufful hugs and throws foes, Ninjask leaves a Shedinja behind, Lapras ferries the team over water, Sableye eats EXP gems, Spiritomb gathers 108 spirits and Polteageist serves tea.
  • Also: Clauncher, Fletchling, Golett, Nacli, Inkay (which evolves upside down), Skorupi, Snover, Tynamo, Dewpider, Duraludon, Bounsweet, Sneasel, Heracross, Croagunk, Sinistea, Impidimp, Pawmi, Rookidee, Elekid and Magby.
  • Only in the Safari Zone, each in its habitat: Kangaskhan, Pinsir, Skarmory, Drilbur, Drampa, Meditite, Numel, Buneary, Flabébé and Skrelp, all with Megas, and Phione, in the Frozen Cove, once Manaphy is yours.
  • Families with a hook of their own, from the Safari too: Vivillon wears the wings of the region you’re in and scatters powders that poison, paralyze or put foes to sleep; Minior drops its shell under half HP and shows its Core, faster and stronger; and Tyrogue evolves by your build: more Attack makes Hitmonlee, more Defense makes Hitmonchan, a tie makes Hitmontop.
  • New prizes at the Game Corner counter: Nidoran♀, Nidoran♂, Cleffa (with Mega Clefable), Plusle and Minun, and each as a Shadow too. In co-op, a Plusle and a Minun on the same team link up in a current that hurts the horde.

新的超级进化

  • On lines already in the game: Mega Steelix, Mega Eelektross, Mega Sableye, Mega Absol and Mega Absol Z, Mega Mawile, Mega Heracross, Mega Abomasnow, Mega Froslass, Mega Scrafty, Mega Chandelure, Mega Golurk, Mega Malamar, Mega Golisopod, Mega Baxcalibur and Mega Tatsugiri.
  • With the new families: Mega Aerodactyl, Mega Kangaskhan, Mega Pinsir, Mega Skarmory, Mega Excadrill, Mega Drampa, Mega Medicham, Mega Camerupt, Mega Lopunny, Mega Floette, Mega Dragalge and Mega Clefable. That’s 66 Megas across 59 lines now.
  • In Legend mode, Mewtwo also becomes Mega Mewtwo X, Zygarde gets its Mega, Diancie and Zeraora awaken Mega at Lv 28 and Dialga awakens Primal.

化石

  • Old Amber now brings Aerodactyl, as in the games, with Mega Aerodactyl.
  • The other fossils come from the Mine Dig and the lab restores them on the spot: Omanyte, Kabuto, Lileep, Anorith, Cranidos, Shieldon, Archen, Tyrunt and Amaura. Galar’s halves wait in the lab until the other one turns up: put two together for Dracozolt, Arctozolt or Dracovish.

地区形态与旅行

  • 42 regional forms from Alola, Galar, Hisui and Paldea, each playing its own way: Alolan Vulpix and Sandshrew, Galarian Meowth, Slowpoke, Zigzagoon, Corsola and Darumaka, Hisuian Growlithe, Zorua, Voltorb and Qwilfish, Paldean Wooper and Tauros, and more. That’s 18 new families, with moves and abilities from their region.
  • Regional evolutions: Pichu, Cubone and Exeggcute can become their Alolan form with the new Sparkling Stone, and Cyndaquil, Oshawott, Rowlet and Goomy their Hisuian form with the Black Augurite. Buy the stone on the shelf or find it on a boss.
  • The Trip: every Monday the trip moves on to the next region. The Travel Egg, in Kecleon’s Showcase, only hatches families from the week’s region, and they visit the Safari Zone too.

火箭队

  • Team Rocket’s Game Corner has opened in the Poké Realm: buy tokens with ₽ (never gems), play the slots and Koffing Sweep, and trade the tokens for prizes at Meowth’s counter.
  • Exclusive prizes: Ditto, which copies a foe; Absol, which foresees disaster; Gimmighoul, which becomes Gholdengo at 999 coins; and Mawile, which bites whoever comes from behind. Absol and Mawile have Megas.
  • Shadow Pokémon: more damage dealt and taken, with a violet aura. Walk with them in your runs to purify them.
  • Rocket Hideout: the Game Corner’s poster opens the way. Go down through the grunts’ floors and face Giovanni’s Persian and Shadow Mewtwo.

狩猎地带、矿坑挖掘与玩偶夹娃娃机

  • The Safari Zone has opened on the Realm’s Trail: walk the tall grass of four reserves, meet Pokémon and throw Safari Balls, berries, rocks and bait. One free session a day, the rest for ₽, and a family you already have pays ₽ back. After 10 sessions without a new family, the next one brings a sure one.
  • Day and night by your own clock, Pokémon visible in the grass to sneak up on, a shiny chain, each habitat’s Pokédex and the Bug-Catching Contest on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, with a weekly ranking.
  • Complete the Frozen Cove Pokédex to earn the Manaphy Egg: it hatches Manaphy, which plays in Legend mode with Heart Swap, and opens Phione in the Safari.
  • Mine Dig, with Excadrill: break a wall open and pull out what’s buried before it collapses. Fossils go to the lab, sometimes a golden wall turns up, and the rarest treasure is the Pink Diamond, out of which Diancie rises.
  • The Plush Claw Machine in the arcade: aim the claw and take a plush home to your base. Every miss nudges the plush toward the chute, and the tenth credit on it always wins.

究极异兽

  • An Ultra Wormhole opens in every run: the sky cracks with prismatic light, you cross Ultra Space and face one of 10 Ultra Beasts, each in its own style, all the way to the Beast Ball.
  • The Ultra Beast of the week shows up more often, and the lobby shows which one it is. Capture all 10 to play them in Legend mode, with Beast Boost, and get Poipole as a gift.

帕路奇亚与地图上的传说

  • Palkia has its own rift, the Folded Space: slabs linked by twin portals, a hall that wraps around, Palkia swapping places with you and its Origin Forme in the last phase. Once captured, it plays in Legend mode with Spacial Rend. Owning Dialga and Palkia earns the Master of Time and Space title.
  • Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf roam the map and flee when you come close. The HP they lose carries over from one run to the next.
  • The Swords of Justice wait in their biomes with a trial: Cobalion of Defense, Terrakion of Strength and Virizion of Speed. Capture all three and Keldeo arrives as a gift.
  • Hoopa’s Rings: the boss rush on the Legends tab. Poké Dollars and the Poké Mart

  • Enemies drop Poké Dollars (₽), and bosses shower them. A bonus for time, fainted foes and winning comes at the end of the run.
  • A Poké Mart with permanent upgrades: HP Up, Protein, Iron, Carbos, Calcium, Zinc, PP Up, Exp. Charm, Amulet Coin, Ability Capsule and Escape Rope.
  • Reroll and Skip on level up.

Move evolutions

  • A maxed move plus the right item becomes an evolved move, like Ember + Charcoal → Flamethrower.
  • 10 evolutions and 7 new type items (Charcoal, Mystic Water, Miracle Seed, Silk Scarf, Magnet, Poison Barb and Sharp Beak).

Shinies and legendary bosses

  • Shiny wild Pokémon (1 in 450) become elites that drop a Poké Ball, and your starter can be shiny (1 in 64).
  • Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres and Mewtwo as bosses with their own attacks. Sometimes Mew shows up at the end.

All 27 starters

  • The starter trio of every region, Kanto to Paldea, with all 3 stages (Lv 12 and Lv 28), their own stats and moves.
  • 13 new moves and the Steel, Dark and Fairy types.
  • A new starter select with region tabs and an inspect panel.

Map items

  • Item Balls, Berry Trees and tall grass around the map. Break them for Potions, Rare Candies, Exp. Share, the Poké Flute, Max Repel, Voltorb, X Attack, X Speed and Revive.

Status conditions

  • Burn, poison and paralysis, like on the handhelds. New held items: King's Rock and Toxic Orb.

Special attack

  • A manual attack on a cooldown (Space, the on-screen button or the gamepad A / ✕): Flare Blitz, Hydro Pump and Grass Knot, which become Blast Burn, Hydro Cannon and Frenzy Plant at the final evolution.

Mobile version

  • A floating touch joystick, an on-screen pause button and layouts for portrait and landscape.
  • Installable as a fullscreen app.

First release

  • Survive 15 minutes of hordes with Charmander, Squirtle or Bulbasaur: automatic moves, level ups, mid-run evolution and bosses.
