FAQ

How do you unlock Tandemaus in Pokémon Survivors?

By catching it in the Poké Realm’s Safari Zone: no egg brings Tandemaus. It lives in one of the habitats, which change every week; catch it once and it's yours to play for good.

How does Tandemaus evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Maushold at Lv 20.

What is Tandemaus's starting move?

Tandemaus starts every run with Population Bomb, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Tandemaus good in Pokémon Survivors?

Tandemaus doesn’t have enough runs yet to get a tier on the tier list. It’s updated over time.
