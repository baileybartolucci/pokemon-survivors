Regigigas in Pokémon Survivors

Regigigas is one of the 62 playable legends in Pokémon Survivors (legendaries, mythicals and Ultra Beasts), a Normal type. Starts slow. Then the strongest of all.

Base values. Regular enemies get tougher every minute; bosses don't.

When it shows up

  • Wakes in the temple that rises between 4:00 and 9:00, only in the runs of whoever captured Regirock, Regice and Registeel
  • Comes back in Hoopa’s Rings (Legend mode) once caught

Attacks

  • Phase 1
    • 1 marked strike on the ground near you every 2.4s (20 damage)
    • Up close: Area slam around itself every 4.8s (24 damage)
    • At range: 3 marked strikes on the ground near you every 4.4s (20 damage)
    • Volley of 3 projectiles aimed at you, with aim lines before it fires, every 6s (20 damage), if you keep away
    • Up close: Ring of 10 projectiles every 5s (18 damage), on Nightmare only
  • Phase 2 (under 66% HP)
    • 1 marked strike on the ground near you every 2.4s (22 damage)
    • At range: 4 marked strikes on the ground near you every 3.6s (20 damage)
    • Ring of 18 projectiles closing on you, with one way out, every 9s (18 damage)
    • At range: 3 beams out of itself, its line blinking first, every 6.5s (20 damage)
    • Up close: Area slam around itself every 4.2s (24 damage)
    • Shuts you in a circle of flames or rocks, every 12s (14 damage), under 50% HP
    • Ultimate Giga Impact, every 18s: Vanishes while its shadow chases you; it stops and it lands there, every 18s (30 damage); Ring of 18 projectiles every 18s (20 damage)
  • Phase 3 (under 33% HP)
    • 1 marked strike on the ground near you every 2.4s (22 damage)
    • Shuts you in a circle of flames or rocks, every 11s (16 damage)
    • At range: 3 parallel lances across the screen, the gaps between them safe, every 6.5s (20 damage)
    • Up close: Area slam around itself every 4s (26 damage)
    • At range: Ring of 14 projectiles every 4s (20 damage)
    • Volley of 5 projectiles aimed at you, with aim lines before it fires, every 4.4s (20 damage), if you keep away, on Nightmare only
    • Ultimate Crush Grip, every 18s: A blast over everything (50 damage), except in the marked shelters; 7 marked strikes on the ground near you every 18s (30 damage)

Legend Mode

  • Capture Regirock, Regice and Registeel: then beat it at the temple that rises in runs.
  • Innate (always on): Slow Start — Starts slow and weak; every 12 s it gains strength, past normal after a minute.
  • Starts with Crush Grip Normal

★ Beating a shiny one on any win, before or after the catch, unlocks its shiny once it’s caught.

FAQ

How do you catch Regigigas in Pokémon Survivors?

Capture Regirock, Regice and Registeel: then beat it at the temple that rises in runs. Beating a shiny one on any win, before or after the catch, unlocks its shiny once it’s caught.
