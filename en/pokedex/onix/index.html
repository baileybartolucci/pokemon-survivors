Onix is one of the families that hatch from eggs in Pokémon Survivors, a Rock and Ground type. Its rock body drags behind you and hurts whatever it touches. Steelix grows longer, and steel.

FAQ

How do you unlock Onix in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Rare Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Onix hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Onix evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Steelix at Lv 20.

What is Onix's starting move?

Onix starts every run with Iron Tail, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Onix Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Steelix. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Onix good in Pokémon Survivors?

Onix doesn’t have enough runs yet to get a tier on the tier list. It’s updated over time.
