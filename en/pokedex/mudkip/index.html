FAQ

How do you unlock Mudkip in Pokémon Survivors?

Mudkip is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Mudkip evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Marshtomp at Lv 12 and into Swampert at Lv 28.

What is Mudkip's starting move?

Mudkip starts every run with Mud Shot, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Mudkip Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Swampert. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Mudkip good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Mudkip is C tier: it beat Mewtwo in 62% of its 556 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
