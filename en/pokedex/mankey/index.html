FAQ

How do you unlock Mankey in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Encounter. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Mankey hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Mankey evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Primeape at Lv 12 and into Annihilape at Lv 28.

What is Mankey's starting move?

Mankey starts every run with Cross Chop, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Mankey good in Pokémon Survivors?

Mankey doesn’t have enough runs yet to get a tier on the tier list. It’s updated over time.
