Base values. Regular enemies get tougher every minute; bosses don't.

When it shows up

  • The weekends' 7★ Stellar Tera Raid: in the Raid of the Day's den, around 13:10. Beat it once to catch it
  • Comes back in Hoopa’s Rings (Legend mode) once caught

Attacks

  • 4 marked strikes along your path, from where you stand to where you're heading, every 3.6s (18 damage)
  • Ring of 12 projectiles every 4.4s (15 damage)
  • Area slam around itself every 6.5s (22 damage)
  • A beam out of itself, its line blinking first, every 7.5s (18 damage)

Legend Mode

  • Beat it once as a boss, in any mode, and it becomes playable in Legend Mode (its own ranking, a tougher horde, awakenings at Lv 12 and 28).
  • Innate (always on): Multiscale — At full HP, you take half damage.
  • Starts with Tera Starstorm Normal

★ Beating a shiny one on any win, before or after the catch, unlocks its shiny once it’s caught.
