FAQ

How do you unlock Wishiwashi in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Encounter. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Wishiwashi hatches, it's yours to play for good.

What is Wishiwashi's starting move?

Wishiwashi starts every run with Water Pulse, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Wishiwashi good in Pokémon Survivors?

Wishiwashi doesn’t have enough runs yet to get a tier on the tier list. It’s updated over time.
