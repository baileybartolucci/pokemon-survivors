FAQ

How do you unlock Meditite in Pokémon Survivors?

By catching it in the Poké Realm’s Safari Zone: no egg brings Meditite. It lives in one of the habitats, which change every week; catch it once and it's yours to play for good.

How does Meditite evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Medicham at Lv 20.

What is Meditite's starting move?

Meditite starts every run with High Jump Kick, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Meditite Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Medicham. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Meditite good in Pokémon Survivors?

Meditite doesn’t have enough runs yet to get a tier on the tier list. It’s updated over time.
