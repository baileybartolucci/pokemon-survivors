Hisuian Zorua and Hisuian Zoroark in Pokémon Survivors

Hisuian Zorua is one of the families that hatch from eggs in Pokémon Survivors, a Normal and Ghost type. A cold spite that saps the crowd, and an illusion that blocks a hit now and then.

FAQ

How do you unlock Hisuian Zorua in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Hisui Travel Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Hisuian Zorua hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Hisuian Zorua evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Hisuian Zoroark at Lv 20.

What is Hisuian Zorua's starting move?

Hisuian Zorua starts every run with Bitter Malice, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Hisuian Zorua good in Pokémon Survivors?

Hisuian Zorua doesn’t have enough runs yet to get a tier on the tier list. It’s updated over time.
